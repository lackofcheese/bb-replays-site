Dice on the ball: Mahaha vs Slam Union

A specialist probability and route report for analysts and developers.

Movement-only sack routes selected by the fixed principal-line shortlist at the start of every defending turn, followed by adaptive evaluation and the tactical verdict. Turns where the active team holds the ball are omitted.

Method and limitations

The search allows up to two setup movers to provide assists, cancel defensive assists or open hitter lanes, and includes basic Frenzy second blocks and crowd-surfs. Generated baselines use the rerolls actually banked at the start of the turn: all-in, hold-one, and hold-two whenever at least two are banked. These are factual commitment ceilings, not tactical verdicts. “Ball down” means the ball is dislodged without a turnover. On verified options, ball-down, turnover and expected-reroll figures price the independently replayed contingent policy; the displayed success spine is shorthand, and its numbered badge opens the state-conditioned branches. Option ranks and the report limit remain a fixed-principal-line shortlist, not a complete adaptive-policy ordering. DE T3's four chain-push options remain fixed-line estimates because recursive Push Back is not yet evaluable. Preparatory blocks, Dump-Off and recovery after a spill are assessed separately.
