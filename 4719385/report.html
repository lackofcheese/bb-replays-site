Jawa brought knives. Dimitri brought a net.

Mahaha 0 – 2 Slam Union

Mahaha conceded, H2T3. Sunny Coast Slam practice, with no lasting consequences.

Very Sunny. Two Assassins. Two Witch Elves. Five stabs. Zero permanent injuries. Somewhere in the glare, Mahaha's murder mystery became a workplace comedy.

Jawa won the toss and kicked. Dimitri answered with no passes and no hurry. The Leader Thrower picked up, and Union began an eight-turn trudge through a cloud of perfume, sharpened steel and terrible intentions.

First half: five shots at the ball

The first removal was almost the player who would decide the match. Slam Union's Frenzy Blitzer followed his block, ate the Dark Elf Wrestle lineman's reply and was knocked out. Dimitri used the apothecary immediately, changing the result to stunned. By the end of the game the Blitzer had made the winning sack and collected the MVP. Quite a recovery from a headache.

Mahaha did not merely rattle the cage. On turn three, Jawa ran a lineman through a missing corner and rolled one die at the ball. Push. Leader reroll. Push. Union's own Wrestle lineman, flat on his back and apparently decorative, occupied the only useful assist square and kept the blitz to one die. A noble calling.

The Union drive stayed alive, but it was never comfortable. The Diving Tackle Blitzer badly hurt a Block Witch Elf and the Leader Thrower escaped west on two rushes. A turn later the ball moved to a Catcher beside the sideline, where a Witch Elf tried to reach him and fell on the way in.

On turn six, a failed Union dodge opened another path to the Catcher. Jawa sent the Witch Elf through: skull, reroll, push, then another push from Frenzy. The Catcher remained standing.

Across the half, five block dice reached Union carriers and none found a knockdown. One Catcher handed to the other, Catch rescued the first roll of 1, and Slam Union walked in for 1-0 on turn eight.

At halftime the assassinations ledger read five stabs, two stuns and no removals. The scoreboard was less kind.

Second half: the net closes

Then came Charge!

Five open Union players moved before Mahaha had an activation. The free blitz managed only a push, but one Assassin was pinned and Union shirts crossed the line. The Diving Tackle Blitzer would soon collect the other Assassin too.

Mahaha's first turn produced three armour breaks and a clean pickup by the Leader Runner. It still ended face-down. An Assassin made his dodge, the Diving Tackle Blitzer objected, and a successful escape became a fall and a stun. On the reply, the preserved Frenzy Blitzer lined up a sideline surf and rolled both-down against a lineman without Block. No crowd required. The lineman was knocked out on the spot, the only removal of the night that stayed removed.

Jawa built a four-corner cage. Dimitri wrapped nine players around it, with the Diving Tackle Blitzer sitting on both prone Assassins. Mahaha broke west for open grass.

Mahaha dismantled both western cage corners and sent the runners ahead. The second Witch Elf then rushed forward and rolled 1. Leader reroll. Another 1. Armour broken. Turn over. The Runner was left behind the broken screen.

The winning counter came in two acts. Dimitri first cleared the Witch Elf and moved five players. The Frenzy Blitzer then came through the gap on two Dodge-covered steps, while both Catchers waited behind him.

The Leader Runner tried to Dump-Off through three tacklezones in Very Sunny weather.

No six.

The pass fumbled. Both-down flattened the Runner. One Catcher picked up the loose ball, rushed and handed it to the other. Nerves of Steel held. Dodge held. One last rush held. The defensive net became a touchdown in eleven seconds.

Two-nil. Five turns remained. Jawa conceded, losing nothing but a practice result and gaining one useful conclusion: for the actual Sunny Coast Slam, Mahaha are out and the Slann are in.

The knives were bad enough. Next time Dimitri gets frogs.
